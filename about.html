About Fernwood Climbing Co.

We opened in the old tannery in 2019, with one wall and a borrowed mat. The building had been empty for eleven years and still smells faintly of leather in August, which regulars insist is part of the charm.

The setting team

Four setters, all of whom climb here on their days off. The rotation means every wall is stripped and reset on a fortnightly cycle, so nothing on the floor is older than two weeks.

Accessibility

Step-free from Fernwood Row through the green side door. The changing rooms and the cafe are on the ground floor. The mezzanine, which holds the training board, is reachable by stairs only.
