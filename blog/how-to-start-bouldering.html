How to start bouldering

Bouldering is climbing without a rope, on walls low enough that falling onto a mat is the normal way down. That is the whole idea. There is no belaying to learn and no partner to find, which is why most people find it the easiest way into climbing.

What to bring

Comfortable clothes you can move in, and nothing else. Shoes and chalk are included when you turn up, and you do not need your own until you are climbing several times a week.

What the grades mean

Indoor bouldering uses the V scale. V0 is the easiest and is designed to be climbable by someone who has never climbed. Most people spend their first few months between V0 and V2, and that is normal rather than slow.

How to fall

Land on your feet, let your knees bend, and roll backwards onto your back. Do not put a hand down to catch yourself — wrists break, backs bounce. The induction covers this properly and takes twenty minutes.
