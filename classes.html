Classes and coaching

Four things run every week. All of them are capped at eight people, and all of them assume you can already fall safely — do the free induction first if you cannot.

Beginner course (four weeks)

Ninety minutes a week for four weeks. Footwork, body position, reading a problem, and enough technique to stop climbing everything with your arms.

Movement clinic

A single two-hour session on one theme — heel hooks, dynos, slab balance — rotating monthly. Suited to V2 and above.

Youth club

Saturdays, ages eight to sixteen, split into two groups by age. Parents do not need to climb but do need to stay in the building.

One-to-one coaching

An hour with a setter, filmed and reviewed. Most useful if you have plateaued at a grade for a few months.
