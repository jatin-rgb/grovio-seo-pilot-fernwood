Indoor bouldering in the old Fernwood tannery

Two hundred problems across forty feet of wall, reset every fortnight by our own setting team. No ropes, no harness, no partner needed — you turn up with shoes and climb. Most people who walk in have never bouldered before.

See day pass prices

What you get

  • Problems graded from V0 to V9, with a dedicated slab wall for first-timers.
  • A full reset every two weeks, so the wall is never the same month to month.
  • Shoe and chalk hire included in every day pass.
  • Free induction session for anyone who has not climbed indoors before.

Where we are

We are in the old tannery building on Fernwood Row, five minutes from the station. Parking is on the street and free after six. The entrance is the green door on the side of the building, not the main shutter.

Opening hours

Weekdays 07:00 to 22:00. Weekends 09:00 to 20:00. The last entry is an hour before close, and the setting team blocks off one wall on Tuesday mornings.
