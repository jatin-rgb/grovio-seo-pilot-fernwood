Do I need to book?

No. Day passes are walk-in and we have never turned anyone away. Classes and inductions do need booking, because group sizes are capped at eight.

Is the induction really free?

Yes, for anyone who has not bouldered indoors before. It runs twenty minutes and covers falling, matting and how the grades work. You still pay for the day pass; the induction itself costs nothing.

Can I pay per month without a contract?

Yes. The monthly membership is rolling. Cancel through your account page or by telling anyone at the desk, and it stops at the end of that month.

Do you do family or group rates?

Groups of six or more get twenty percent off day passes if you tell us in advance. There is no separate family ticket.
